# Assignment: Nonlinear Equation Root-Finding

Select a nonlinear equation relevant to your field of study. Then, using all the methods you've learned in open methods, complete the following:

## Tasks

* **Root Solution:** Find the root solution of the equation using each method.
* **Error Comparison:** Compare the approximation errors for all methods.
* **Comparative Analysis:** Conduct a comparative analysis that considers both the errors and the convergence of each method.
* **Method Selection & Justification:** Determine the most suitable method for your specific equation, considering factors such as the speed of convergence, precision, and other relevant criteria in your field of study. Provide a justification for your choice.
